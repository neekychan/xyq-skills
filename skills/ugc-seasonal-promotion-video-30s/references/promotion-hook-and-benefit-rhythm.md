# Promotion Hook And Benefit Rhythm

Campaign videos prioritize promotion benefit over product education.

## Mandatory Visual Hooks

Pick one. A visual hook can include a visible creator, product, verified gifts, coupon cards, and mechanism cards in the same frame when that improves drama and trust.

- `福利奇观`: creator stands/sits behind or beside a table/shelf/suitcase/drawer/vanity as it fills with main product plus verified gifts; or use a clean product-only table if no creator is needed.
- `赠品摆开`: creator places verified gifts one by one beside the main product; or hands place gifts while the creator reacts in the background.
- `表情包反应`: creator reacts with meme-style shock while product + offer card is already visible, then camera cuts/pushes to gifts or price.
- `夸张剧情`: safe comedic campaign drama, such as boss accidentally releasing deal, friends rushing in, suitcase bursting open.
- `从天而降福利`: verified gifts/coupon cards/product packs drop into basket/table in controlled stylized way, with creator dodging, catching, or pointing in the same scene when useful.
- `0.01元权益`: verified 0.01/1-cent/trial/reservation/lock-benefit action.
- `到手量暴击`: clear buy-X-get-Y, final quantity, bundle reveal.
- `券包砸脸`: coupon/subsidy/member benefit cards stack beside product while creator points to the stacking cards or reacts.
- `限时抢跑`: countdown, deadline, limited gift, live-exclusive card.

## Case Diversity

When outputting two or more cases, create different concepts, not only different wording.

```text
Case A can be: creator reaction + gift pile + price shock.
Case B can be: countdown rush + coupon-card stack + live-room CTA.
Case C can be: unboxing overflow + buy-gift quantity + product-card CTA.
Case D can be: 0.01 verified benefit + reservation action + bundle reveal.
```

Across cases, vary opening image, creator timing, persona, scene, benefit order, transition rhythm, and CTA route. Give each case a fresh first-8-second structure, for example `达人震惊同框福利堆 -> 价格卡`, `倒计时冲刺 -> 券卡叠加`, or `开箱溢出赠品 -> 到手量暴击`.

## 30s Script Structure

```text
0-3s: Hook. Use one visual hook or oral hook from the hook library. Product and campaign atmosphere are visible.
3-7s: Brightest offer. Say the single strongest price, mechanism, coupon, gift, final quantity, 0.01 benefit, or live-room benefit.
7-11s: Mechanism or gift reveal. Make the benefit visible through gift placement, coupon stack, price card, bundle reveal, or benefit card.
11-15s: Product proof 1. Use one sentence and one visual action to show function, effect, specification, taste, texture, capacity, scenario, or use result.
15-19s: Product proof 2 or use scenario. Add a different proof or real-life scenario that supports why the deal is worth taking.
19-23s: Campaign time + offer path. Say the activity time/deadline and how to get the offer.
23-27s: Urgency/trust reminder. Use deadline, limited gift, stock, official benefit, fast delivery, or evidence-backed reason.
27-30s: One CTA. Return to product + offer sticker, give one purchase action.
```

## Product Proof Sentence

Product proof must be product-specific and compact. Use two different proof lines across the 30s video:

```text
护肤：质地/肤感/使用部位/功效方向，一句话。
日用品：规格/抽数/容量/使用频率/收纳便利，一句话。
食品：口味/分量/产地/新鲜度/食用场景，一句话。
家电：功能/效率/空间/前后对比，一句话。
服饰：上身效果/面料/场景，一句话。
3C：参数/续航/速度/场景，一句话。
```

The product proof supports the deal value. It should not become a long product education segment.

## Voiceover Progression

Use eight different speech functions. Each line has a different opening, verb, and purpose.

```text
Beat 1: Hook sentence. Example openings: 这场面..., 先别划..., 我以为看错了..., 这波福利...
Beat 2: Brightest offer sentence. Example openings: 真正狠的是..., 这单核心是..., 价格/机制直接到...
Beat 3: Mechanism or gift reveal sentence. Example openings: 再看赠品..., 这一套还多..., 机制是...
Beat 4: Product proof sentence 1. Example openings: 关键它..., 这个规格..., 用起来/吃起来/穿上...
Beat 5: Product proof or scenario sentence 2. Example openings: 日常..., 放在..., 出门/通勤/囤货...
Beat 6: Time + path sentence. Example openings: 活动到..., 这几天..., 先去..., 进直播间...
Beat 7: Urgency/trust sentence. Example openings: 这波限时..., 赠品有限..., 现货速发..., 官方权益...
Beat 8: CTA sentence. Example openings: 快点击下方链接..., 快来直播间..., 先领券再拍..., 看准这套...
```

Use concrete numbers, rights, product facts, time, and actions. The brightest offer appears once as the main offer; final CTA can reference it with new action wording.

## Product-Specific Script Variation

For different products, change the script idea, not only the nouns.

- Beauty/skincare: hook can be gift pile, texture close-up, meme shock at gift quantity; proof is texture, use feeling, anti-aging/repair/brightening direction if evidence exists.
- Daily goods: hook can be full-table stock-up, drawer/shelf overflow, family/dorm use scene; proof is count, softness, safety, storage, or daily-use convenience.
- Food/snacks: hook can be tasting reaction, table full of packs, office/dorm sharing; proof is flavor, texture, portion, freshness, or origin.
- Appliances/home: hook can be old-vs-new moment, family decision, subsidy card; proof is function, efficiency, size, noise, cleaning, or before-after.
- Fashion/accessories: hook can be outfit switch, mirror reveal, occasion drama; proof is fit, fabric, color, matching scene.
- 3C/digital: hook can be coupon/subsidy stack, speed test, upgrade reaction; proof is parameter, battery, performance, compatibility, or scenario.
