# Screenshot And Phone UI Rules

User-provided screenshots may appear directly, but they use a different container from phone screens.

## Screenshot Containers

User-provided screenshot assets appear as independent evidence layers:

- `independent screenshot card`
- `picture-in-picture evidence card`
- `tabletop printed screenshot card`
- `full-screen evidence insert`
- `floating rectangular reference card with border and shadow`

The screenshot card sits outside any phone frame. Use border, shadow, and safe margins so the screenshot reads as an intentional evidence layer.

## Phone Screen Containers

Phone screens show one simplified, platform-neutral commerce card:

- simplified product card
- simplified coupon card
- simplified order/price card
- simplified benefit card
- simplified CTA card

Phone front screen faces the camera and stays stable vertically or on a table/stand. The display sits inside one visible phone bezel as a flat luminous screen with short readable offer text. The surrounding scene remains a real product table, creator desk, home scene, gift table, brand counter, or simple commerce desk.

## Platform-Neutral UI Principle

Use neutral cards and physical commerce props instead of full platform interface simulation.

- Use `product card`, `coupon card`, `price card`, `benefit card`, `CTA card`, or `phone with one simplified card`.
- Use real product/gift/creator scenes as the main environment.
- Use short offer text and simple icons on cards, not platform-branded app layouts.
- Platform-specific purchase wording can appear in voiceover or a small CTA card when the user provides that path.

## Composition Methods

- `Screenshot-card`: user-provided screenshot asset -> independent flat reference card with border/shadow -> direct quick cut to real product or gift table.
- `Evidence-to-card`: screenshot fact -> short offer sticker -> product/gift shot.
- `Phone-bridge`: real product on table -> direct quick cut to stable front-facing phone on stand showing one simplified product/coupon/benefit card -> direct quick cut back to product/gift table.
- `Mechanism-card`: product close-up -> rack focus to independent price/coupon card in a blank safe zone -> push-in to product and CTA.
- `Commerce-desk-card`: creator/product desk -> independent product card or benefit card beside product -> product stays visible while CTA sticker appears.

## Seedance 2.0 Wording

```text
用户截图作为独立截图卡出现，带边框和阴影，位于产品旁边的安全区域。
手机屏幕展示重新排版的简化商品卡，不承载原始截图。
截图卡展示后，直接快切回真实产品和赠品桌面。
手机固定在桌面支架上，正面展示简化券页，随后快切回产品。
画面主体是实拍商品桌、赠品桌、达人桌面或居家场景；优惠信息以独立机制卡、商品卡、优惠卡、CTA卡呈现。
平台购买路径用中性卡片表达，如“商品卡”“直播间优惠”“领券购买”“下方链接”，画面保持实物场景和单张卡片结构。
```
