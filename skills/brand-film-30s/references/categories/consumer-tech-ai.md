# Consumer Tech and AI Brand Films

## Core Tensions

- Invisible capability, invisible risk, invisible time cost.
- Users fear being watched, replaced, overwhelmed, interrupted, or left behind.
- Technology should give control, not just novelty.

## Modern Routes

- Turn an invisible technical truth into a visible monster, assistant, mirror, shortcut, or world rule.
- Use genre film: horror for surveillance, thriller for security, sci-fi for possibility, comedy for AI mistakes, everyday epic for productivity.

## Product Role

- Product as shield, lens, collaborator, time compressor, memory keeper, or creative tool.

## Avoid

- UI tour.
- Abstract "future is here" visuals.
- AI claims that show laziness, deception, or loss of human care.
