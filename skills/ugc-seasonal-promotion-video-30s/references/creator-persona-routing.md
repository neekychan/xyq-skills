# Creator Persona Routing

Decide whether a visible creator is needed based on the script concept. Creator persona must fit product category, price tier, buyer role, use scenario, campaign occasion, and promotion path when a creator appears.

## Creator Presence Decision

Use visible creator beats for:

- oral recommendation
- live-room style
- meme reaction
- try-on
- review
- demonstration
- character-driven hook
- gift pile reveal with creator reaction
- creator arranging gifts, coupons, or product packs
- deal-hunter pointing to price/coupon/gift mechanism cards
- unboxing, holding, stacking, or comparing products and gifts

Use product-only beats when the shot is stronger without a face, or when the beat needs a clean evidence/product close-up:

- gift pile
- coupon mechanism
- screenshot card
- tabletop flat lay
- package close-up
- bundle reveal
- pure product visual hook

Gift pile, coupon mechanism, tabletop flat lay, package close-up, bundle reveal, and product proof are not required to be product-only. They can be creator-in-frame when the creator adds emotion, urgency, trust, or drama. If a creator appears in the video, consider placing the creator in 0-2s or 2-5s with the product/gifts visible together.

When a beat includes a visible creator, specify `人物呈现`: face, hair, makeup, accessories, clothing, posture, action, expression, and scene detail. Keep the same identity, outfit, hair, and makeup within one video, while changing expression and gesture according to the script task. When a beat is product-only, write `人物呈现：无人物出镜，产品/赠品/机制卡为主体`.

## Persona-Fit Expression Variation

Use visible expression variation when the creator appears in multiple beats. Expressions are generated from persona type, product category, buyer motivation, use scenario, campaign occasion, and script task.

Persona-fit examples:

- Premium beauty/skincare/fragrance: restrained surprise, elegant confidence, careful texture observation, soft satisfied close.
- Practical beauty/personal care: deal-hunter excitement, focused application, mirror-check satisfaction, clear reminder.
- Household/daily goods: practical excitement, quantity-checking focus, storage/use approval, family-use satisfaction.
- Food/snacks: curiosity, tasting delight, sharing excitement, stock-up approval.
- 3C/appliance: focused evaluation, serious parameter proof, upgrade satisfaction, decisive nod.
- Fashion/accessories: outfit confidence, mirror-check approval, scenario imagination, stylish close.
- Gift buyer/relationship: anticipation, heart-flutter, warm approval, gentle close.
- Parent/caregiver: gentle concern, safety-check focus, relieved approval.
- Pet owner: playful curiosity, amused reaction, warm practical approval.

For each beat, write expression as a concrete visible action: eye movement, brow, smile, nod, lean, pause, hand gesture, gaze target, and reaction intensity.

## Benefit And Occasion Persona Fit

Creator styling, expression, clothing, background, props, and rhythm adapt to the primary benefit route and campaign occasion.

Benefit-route fit:

- Live-room traffic / 直播间引流: host or deal guide, clean live-room top, product desk, phone/product-card bridge, fast urgent eyes, forward lean, pointing to live-room/product-card path.
- Gift mechanism / 赠品机制: unboxing or gift-table creator, warmer/refined outfit, vanity/gift table, excited reveal face, delighted brow raise, hands place gifts one by one.
- Discount/direct price / 打折直降: practical deal hunter, sharper expression, price-card desk, coupon card, confident nod, decisive pointing gesture.
- Coupon/full reduction / 领券满减: platform benefit finder, phone-on-stand scene, focused checking expression, clear path gesture.
- 0.01/low-price lock / 0.01锁权益: urgent benefit finder, energetic reaction, reservation/lock card, quick hand point, alert expression.
- Stock-up / 囤货: family buyer, dorm user, home organizer, casual clothes, storage shelf/kitchen/bathroom scene, practical excitement, quantity-checking focus.
- Festival gifting / 节日礼赠: gift buyer, polished outfit, gift table/date/holiday background, soft anticipation, heart-flutter, warm approval.
- Brand/member day / 品牌日会员权益: refined or official host, brand counter/live desk, restrained premium styling, trust-building confidence, calm explanation.
- New-season/changeover / 换季上新: commuter/travel/outdoor user, seasonal clothing and background, immediate-use expression, refreshing or practical satisfaction.

Occasion fit:

- 618/99/双11/双12: high-energy deal room, coupon/gift table, fast urgency, deal-hunter expression.
- 七夕/520/情人节: softer romantic/gift background, polished styling, anticipation and warm smile.
- 年货节/春节不打烊: family/home hosting background, warm red/gold accents, stock-up satisfaction.
- 开学季: dorm/desk/backpack scene, student or parent role, practical preparation expression.
- 品牌日/会员日: official brand desk/counter, refined trust expression, member-card or points prop.
- 换季/夏日/秋冬: season-specific clothing, outdoor/commute/vanity refresh scene, immediate-use expression.

## Story-Skin Emotion Routing

For story-skin hooks such as 剧情, 短剧, 富二代, 霸总, 反转, 逆袭, or 玩梗, define the character emotion arc together with persona:

- setup emotion: doubt, being ignored, playful arrogance, awkwardness, misunderstanding, or low-status frustration
- reversal emotion: surprise, realization, jealousy, regret, impressed reaction, sudden interest, or heart-flutter
- proof emotion: focused belief, careful confirmation, sincere approval
- action emotion: urgency, quick decision, deal-hunter excitement
- ending emotion: satisfaction,爽感,得意,释然, encouraging close

If there are two characters, assign different but connected reactions. Example: 富二代 starts confident/aloof, then surprised and impressed by product effect; main creator starts ignored or calm, then confident and satisfied after revealing the campaign benefit.

## Role-Based Expression Fit

When multiple people appear, write role-specific personas instead of one shared creator persona.

- 主角: the product user, recommender, deal finder, or person who wins the reversal. Expression should support product value: calm setup, confident reveal, focused proof, urgent guidance, satisfied close.
- 配角: the person who misunderstands, doubts, challenges, admires, regrets, or becomes convinced. Expression should support the story conflict: skeptical/arrogant/confused setup, surprised reversal, interested or regretful proof beat, quick decision, approving close.
- 旁观者: brief curiosity, surprise, agreement, or shared excitement; keep them visually secondary.

Role examples:

- 富二代配角: aloof raised brow -> surprised eye contact -> impressed half-smile -> decisive nod.
- 霸总配角: restrained control -> sudden attention -> serious evaluation -> approval nod.
- 闺蜜配角: teasing doubt -> widened eyes -> excited lean-in -> shared smile.
- 室友配角: casual disbelief -> grabs attention -> practical approval -> urge to buy.
- 主角逆袭人设: ignored/calm -> confident reveal -> focused demonstration ->爽感 smile.

## Persona Variables

Specify:

- age bracket: 18-24, 25-35, 30-45, 40-55
- gender or relationship: woman, man, neutral practical buyer, couple, mother-daughter, parent-child, spouse, roommate, colleague, pet owner, live host
- face temperament: friendly round face, refined oval face, practical square face, mature calm face, sweet, cool, energetic, professional, homey, premium
- hairstyle and hair color: low ponytail, loose hair, short hair, bun, bangs, brown waves, clean short cut, cap hairstyle
- makeup: no-makeup look, commute light makeup, refined date makeup, professional workplace makeup, outdoor sunscreen makeup, mature premium makeup
- accessories: earrings, necklace, watch, glasses, hair clip, scarf, tote bag, sports bag, parent bag, pet leash
- clothing: shirt, knit top, homewear, suit jacket, sports jacket, dress, apron, sun-protection jacket, hoodie, live-room clean top
- scene: bathroom vanity, kitchen, living room, laundry area, office, dorm, camping table, travel suitcase, pet corner, baby room, live-room desk, brand counter
- delivery state: friend-like reveal, deal hunter, calm review, home stock-up, gift recommendation, live-room urgent reminder, meme shock reaction, professional review
- expression arc: surprise/curiosity, confidence, focused proof, urgency, encouraging close

## Category Fit

- Beauty/skincare/fragrance: refined self-use, commuter woman, gift buyer, clean makeup, vanity/gift table.
- Household/daily goods: family buyer, home organizer, kitchen/bathroom/laundry scene.
- 3C/appliance: upgrade buyer, digital reviewer, desk/home setup, practical tone.
- Food/snacks: family buyer, office sharer, dorm stock-up, tasting reaction.
- Baby/parenting: parent/caregiver, gentle handling, baby room/home scene.
- Pet: pet owner, pet reaction, bowl/toy/litter scene.
- Fashion/accessories: try-on creator, commute/travel/date scene, full-body proof.

## Batch Rotation

For multiple products or multiple case outputs, rotate at least four dimensions:

- opening hook type: gift pile, meme reaction, coupon-card stack, 0.01 verified benefit, countdown rush, unboxing overflow, price-card slap, live-room desk
- creator presence timing: creator appears at 0-2s, creator appears at 2-5s, creator appears throughout, product-only with hands, product-only with mechanism cards
- creator persona: age, role, styling, makeup, expression, and delivery state
- scene: vanity table, live-room desk, bathroom counter, gift table, suitcase, office desk, dorm table, home storage corner
- camera rhythm: push-in, pull-back, pan across gifts, rack focus to coupon card, match cut, quick cut from reaction to gift reveal
- benefit order: price first, coupon first, gift first, quantity first, limited-time first
- CTA path: product card, live room, coupon first, yellow cart, reservation/0.01 lock if verified

Two generated cases should not share the same first 8 seconds. Keep one persona consistent within one video.

## Persona Revision

When the user modifies creator persona, update:

- `达人画像`
- every visible creator beat's `人物呈现`
- scene and action if the new persona changes context
- voice tone if the new persona changes delivery style

Preserve product, offer, and script facts unless the user also asks to change them.
