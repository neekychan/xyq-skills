# Genre Routing

## Short-Drama Genre Fit

For 剧情向带货 or 短剧带货, choose the short-drama genre before writing the product story. Do not jump from product feature to usage scenario. The genre determines why the audience watches; the product determines how the protagonist wins.

Use this mapping as a starting point, then adapt it to the specific product:

| Short-drama genre | Core audience and fantasy | Common pressure relationship | Product should function as |
|---|---|---|---|
| 女频逆袭 | Women who enjoy being underestimated, then becoming admired, beautiful, capable, or independent | ex, rival, mother-in-law, boss, classmate, husband, relatives | transformation proof, status signal, confidence trigger, competence evidence |
| 霸道总裁 / 甜宠 | Young women who enjoy chosen-by-him, protected, cared-for, identity-gap romance | CEO, heroine, love rival, family opposition, workplace gossip | caring gift, intimate detail, identity clue, relationship-softening object |
| 重生复仇 | Audiences who enjoy regret reversal, "this time I know better", and preemptive counterattack | former betrayer, family, workplace rival, bad investment, wrong partner | correct choice this time, avoided trap, early advantage, proof of foresight |
| 男频逆袭 | Men who enjoy being humiliated, then proving money, ability, strength, or status | boss, relatives, classmates, competitors, ex, customer | skill weapon, resource amplifier, earning proof, equipment upgrade |
| 赘婿 / 战神 / 隐藏身份 | Audiences who enjoy hidden power and public face-slap | in-laws, family banquet, arrogant rich rival, wife being bullied | identity token, hidden resource, crisis-solving tool, proof of real status |
| 家庭伦理 | Parents, spouses, caregivers, and older audiences who enjoy grievance, sacrifice, misunderstanding, and family recognition | mother-in-law, spouse, child, parent, sibling, neighbor | care evidence, labor-saving proof, health/safety proof, love made visible |
| 职场爽剧 | White-collar, creator, merchant, and professional audiences who enjoy deadline pressure and public competence proof | boss, client, senior colleague, team rival, investor | efficiency weapon, professional standard, result proof, deadline reversal |
| 悬疑反转 / 泰式误会 | Broad audience that enjoys being misled by a serious-looking but absurd situation | family, neighbors, bystanders, police-like authority, partner | final reveal key that recontextualizes every prior abnormal behavior |
| 喜剧短剧 | Audience that enjoys embarrassment, social misunderstanding, and exaggerated daily friction | friend group, roommate, partner, gym, office, family | cause of misunderstanding or elegant fix to a silly recurring problem |

### Genre-First Product Placement

After choosing the genre, assign the product one clear plot role. Do not let it be a generic sponsor prop.

- **Reversal evidence**: proves the protagonist was right, capable, prepared, or not what others assumed.
- **Status signal**: changes how others read the protagonist's taste, class, professionalism, beauty, or discipline.
- **Secret weapon**: lets the protagonist survive a deadline, competition, humiliation, or impossible task.
- **Care object**: shows that one character understands another person's hidden need.
- **Wrong-choice correction**: the protagonist previously suffered because of the wrong product/method; this product is the smarter second choice.
- **Final reveal key**: the product appears late and explains all earlier strange behavior.
- **Relationship repair proof**: turns accusation into recognition because the product exposes effort, care, or sacrifice.

Reject a story route when the product can be swapped with any other item without changing the protagonist's win. That is a loose skit, not product-integrated short drama.

### Content Route Selection

Before writing the script, choose what kind of short-drama content the product deserves. Do not pick a genre because it is popular; pick it because the product can own the reversal proof in that genre.

If more than one route is viable for the user's product and request, do not make the final choice silently. Prepare 2-4 concise route candidates and hand the final decision to the Creative Direction Questionnaire Gate in `SKILL.md`. The route selector may recommend one option, but the user must choose before the agent writes the final creative plan markdown, final script, or `video_generation_prompt`.

Use this route selector:

- **If the product visibly improves appearance, taste, style, or social perception**, use 女频逆袭, Social Flex, or status reversal. The爽点 is "they underestimated her, then everyone sees she made the better choice."
- **If the product is giftable, intimate, comforting, or shows attention to detail**, use 甜宠, family recognition, or relationship repair. The爽点 is "someone finally noticed what she actually needed."
- **If the product saves time, prevents mistakes, improves output, or gives a measurable result**, use 职场爽剧, efficiency crush, or expert test. The爽点 is "the person under pressure produces the result while others panic or fail."
- **If the product solves a small hidden daily problem that causes visible awkwardness**, use comedy misunderstanding or Thai-style delayed reveal. The爽点 is "everyone misread the behavior until the tiny cause is revealed."
- **If the product corrects a common bad choice**, use rebirth/regret reversal or wrong-choice correction. The爽点 is "this time she knows better and avoids the old loss."
- **If the product is a status-coded object**, use hidden identity, social flex, or public recognition. The爽点 is "people misread her level until the object exposes the truth."
- **If the product is routine or low-drama**, build pressure from the scene, not from the product: family gathering, group photo, urgent client task, public comparison, travel emergency, date, school dorm, office deadline, neighbor gossip, or live shopping decision.

The chosen content route should answer three questions before scripting:

1. What is the audience's familiar life pressure?
2. Who misjudges or tests the protagonist in public?
3. What can the product prove that a normal prop cannot prove?

If those three answers are weak, do not write the script yet. Pick a sharper route or a narrower audience.

### Satisfying Reversal Upgrade

When the user wants the idea to feel "爽", do not settle for a gentle lifestyle improvement. Upgrade the scene into an immediate public win.

The usual failure is: the product makes life better, but nobody loses face, no pressure is at stake, and the audience has no one to root against. To add short-drama satisfaction, strengthen four elements before rewriting:

- **Clear antagonist**: give the viewer one person or group who misjudges, humiliates, blocks, or tests the protagonist. This can be a boss, rival, ex, classmate, client, relative, bystander, or smug colleague.
- **Immediate stakes**: make the pressure time-bound and consequential: lose the project, miss the order, get replaced, be exposed, fail the test, disappoint the family, or lose public respect.
- **Product as the winning move**: the product should not merely make the protagonist comfortable. It must create the decisive proof, exact information, visible result, or calm state that lets the protagonist win.
- **On-the-spot face-slap**: the person who looked down on the protagonist should witness the reversal in the same scene. The payoff should be public, specific, and emotionally legible.

Upgrade pattern:

**soft pain -> hard pressure -> hostile witness -> product-enabled proof -> antagonist error exposed -> public recognition -> value line**

For example, for noise-cancelling headphones, "commuting feels quieter" is weak. A stronger short-drama version is: noisy office + rival deliberately disrupts + protagonist must repeat the client's exact requirement in ten minutes + headphones let her hear the original voice note clearly + rival pitches the wrong point + client confirms she is the only one who understood. The product is not comfort; it is the reason she wins.

Use this upgrade especially for 职场爽剧, 女频逆袭, 男频逆袭, 赘婿/战神, 重生复仇, and any physical or digital product that can prove competence, taste, speed, accuracy, or preparedness.

### Script Planning For 爽点

Plan the script around a rising chain of misjudgment, not around product features. The audience feels爽 when they can clearly see four things:

1. **Wrong judgment**: someone makes a fast, unfair, or arrogant judgment about the protagonist.
2. **Pressure lock**: the protagonist cannot simply leave; there is a deadline, public gaze, relationship obligation, money at stake, status at stake, or emotional debt.
3. **Quiet preparation**: the protagonist knows something, owns something, or has made a smarter choice that others do not understand yet.
4. **Visible reversal**: the proof appears in the same scene, in front of the person who misjudged them.

Use this beat logic:

**misread -> pressure -> escalation -> hidden choice -> proof -> witness reaction -> value sentence**

Good爽点 is not just "the product worked." It is "the antagonist's earlier judgment is exposed as wrong." Make the earlier judgment specific enough that the payoff can answer it directly.

Examples:

- If the antagonist says "you look too conservative," the proof should be a group photo where she looks the most natural and polished.
- If the boss says "you cannot finish in ten minutes," the proof should be the completed deliverable landing before the deadline.
- If the family says "you bought another useless thing," the proof should be the product solving the exact family crisis they are facing.
- If the friend says "this looks cheap," the proof should be a stranger, client, or date recognizing the product's taste or result.

Avoid false爽点:

- The protagonist explains why the product is good, but nobody changes their mind.
- The antagonist disappears before the payoff.
- The product appears after the conflict is already solved.
- The reversal depends on vague beauty, confidence, or "高级感" instead of a visible reaction.
- The product could be replaced by any other product without changing the win.

### Product-To-Drama Fit Questions

Before writing, answer these in order:

1. Which short-drama genre does this TA likely already watch?
2. Which content route best matches what the product can prove?
3. What exact pressure line would that genre use in the first 3 seconds?
4. Who is misjudging, humiliating, doubting, or testing the protagonist?
5. What hidden information or smarter choice does the protagonist have?
6. What plot function can the product own?
7. What visible proof makes the antagonist or bystander change their mind?
8. What one conversion sentence naturally follows the emotional payoff?

## Optional Platform Check

Platform research should support the short-drama decision, not replace it. Use it only when the product is trend-sensitive, the user asks for market evidence, or the likely audience/scene is unclear.

When checking platform or competitor references, only extract information that changes the script:

- Which audience pressure appears repeatedly in comments, saves, reviews, or creator posts?
- Which scenes make the product feel believable: family dinner, group photo, office deadline, date, dorm, commute, travel, live room, or public comparison?
- What objections or anxieties should the antagonist say out loud?
- What product proof must appear before the conversion line?
- What phrase should be repeated across the series for search, cart, or live-room memory?

Do not let platform playbooks dictate the script. TikTok, Douyin, Xiaohongshu, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest, and other platform references are useful only when they sharpen the audience pressure, the opening misjudgment, or the visible proof.

## Short-Drama Selling Modes

Pick one selling mode per video. The mode is not a generic ad formula; it is the exact reason the product can create the short-drama payoff.

### 1. Public Reversal

Use when the product can visibly change how others judge the protagonist's beauty, taste, competence, preparation, or status.

- Core emotion: being underestimated, then winning in front of the same people.
- Structure: public misjudgment -> pressure lock -> protagonist stays quiet -> product proof appears -> antagonist's judgment is exposed -> public recognition -> value line.
- Product role: reversal evidence, status signal, or confidence trigger.
- Hook patterns: "所有人都以为她穿错了，直到合照出来。"; "老板当众说她十分钟做不出来就走人。"

### 2. Relationship Recognition

Use when the product can show that someone noticed a hidden need, defended the protagonist, repaired a misunderstanding, or made care visible.

- Core emotion: finally being seen, chosen, protected, or understood.
- Structure: protagonist is blamed or overlooked -> relationship pressure grows -> product appears as care/proof -> accusation softens -> relationship payoff -> value line.
- Product role: caring gift, relationship repair proof, intimate detail, or family-care evidence.
- Hook patterns: "她以为他忘了，直到盒子打开。"; "婆婆说她乱花钱，下一秒全家都沉默了。"

### 3. Deadline / Competence Win

Use when the product saves time, prevents mistakes, improves output, or creates a measurable result under pressure.

- Core emotion: others panic or doubt, but the protagonist delivers.
- Structure: impossible task -> hostile witness raises stakes -> product-enabled action -> result lands before deadline -> witness reaction -> value line.
- Product role: secret weapon, professional standard, efficiency weapon, or exact-information proof.
- Hook patterns: "客户十分钟后到，老板却把烂摊子丢给她。"; "所有人都在返工，只有他提前交了。"

### 4. Misunderstanding Reveal

Use when the product solves a small hidden problem that causes visible awkwardness, strange behavior, or social misreading.

- Core emotion: curiosity, comedy, delayed understanding, warm reversal.
- Structure: audience misreads behavior -> misunderstanding escalates -> true cause appears -> product reframes earlier scenes -> humane punchline -> value line.
- Product role: final reveal key, wrong-choice correction, or elegant fix.
- Hook patterns: "全家都以为她变了，只有最后一秒才知道原因。"; "她越解释，事情越离谱。"

### 5. Wrong-Choice Correction / Rebirth (重生)

Use when the product corrects a common mistake, regret, cheap substitute, bad habit, or outdated method. This mode is the natural fit for the **重生** genre.

- Core emotion: this time she/he knows better; foresight, regret reversal, preemptive win.
- Structure: old choice caused loss or embarrassment -> similar pressure returns -> protagonist chooses differently -> product prevents the old loss -> witness reaction -> value line.
- Product role: smarter second choice, avoided trap, or regret reversal.
- Hook patterns: "去年她就是在这里丢了脸，今年她只换了一个选择。"; "她这次没解释，直接把结果拿出来。"

#### 重生 Genre Hook Formula

The standard rebirth short-drama hook uses the **"上一世 [具体惨痛经历]，这一世 [决心/行动]"** structure. This is the genre's signature opening — audiences instantly recognize the format and enter the "preemptive revenge" expectation.

**Canonical structure**:

> 黑白/冷色闪回（1-2秒）：上一世的惨痛画面 + 旁白/字幕："上一世，[具体失败/被骂/丢脸经历]。"
>
> 切彩色/暖色正片：主角回到同一场景，表情淡定 + 旁白/字幕："这一世，[我不xxx了 / 我要xxx]。"

**Key rules**:

- "上一世"必须具体到一个可视化的失败瞬间，不能抽象（"上一世我不够好"太弱；"上一世我跑出去买酒迟到，项目被抢了"够具体）。
- "这一世"用短句，表达决心或反向行动（"我不跑了"、"我先下手"、"该花的一分不省"）。
- 视觉上用黑白/冷色 vs 彩色/暖色对比，让观众1秒区分"过去"和"现在"。
- 音效上，闪回用急促心跳或模糊人声，正片切入时突然安静或换成自信BGM。
- The "上一世/这一世" line can appear as voiceover, on-screen text, or both. Keep each half under 15 characters for readability on mobile.

**Hook examples for commerce**:

- "上一世，我跑出去买酒，回来项目没了。这一世，我不跑了。"（即时配送）
- "上一世，我省了这笔钱，结果赔了十倍。这一世，该花的一分不省。"（品质消费）
- "上一世，我信了柜姐推荐，烂脸三个月。这一世，我只信成分。"（护肤）
- "上一世，我穿错了那条裙子，合照被嘲了一年。这一世，我知道该选什么。"（服装）
- "上一世，我没带这个，整个旅程毁了。这一世，出门第一件事就是它。"（旅行用品）
- "上一世，我加班到凌晨三点还没做完。这一世，十分钟搞定。"（效率工具）
- "上一世，我在婆婆面前丢了脸。这一世，我让她先闭嘴。"（家庭场景产品）
- "上一世，我在他面前哭着求复合。这一世，我让他求我。"（变美/自信类）
- "上一世，我请客拿不出像样的酒。这一世，15分钟全到齐。"（酒水/礼品）

**Rebirth script structure for 15s**:

```
0-3s: 黑白闪回抓人，直接给上一世失败的最痛画面；切彩色回到同一场景，旁白"这一世，[短决心]"，压力角色或见证者出现
3-7s: 对手/场景即将重演上一世的转折点，主角表情和视线变稳；主角淡定行动（下单/选择/准备），产品或关键选择开始出现
7-12s [PRODUCT]: 产品结果扩大成可见证明，说明"这一世的选择"比上一世早/好/准
12-15s: 上一世的失败点变成这一世的胜利点，对手表情凝固，主角举杯/微笑/淡定离开
```

**Series application**: In a rebirth series, each episode "rebirths" to a different life scene where the same product would have prevented a specific loss. The "上一世/这一世" formula stays consistent across episodes, but the scene and stakes rotate. This creates a recognizable series signature that audiences follow.

#### Ancient / Qinggong / Rebirth Modern-Product Reversal

Use this route when the story uses 古装剧、清宫剧、宫斗剧、轻功武侠、仙侠、架空古代, or 重生爽剧 to sell a modern product or service. The modern product appearing inside an ancient world is not a continuity error. It can be the highest-weight creative premise when it creates a stronger memory hook and a clearer reversal.

The rule is:

**ancient power scene -> public humiliation or impossible court/banquet/family pressure -> modern product appears as a serious plot weapon -> ancient witnesses react -> product proof completes the face-slap**

Treat 古今错位 as the main visual engine, not a small gag. If the concept is "清宫宫斗 + modern delivery", the phone, app page, courier, delivery bag, order-complete state, and delivered product must be key evidence. The ancient characters should treat the modern object seriously because it decides the social outcome.

High-fit scenes:

- **清宫宫斗带货**: 掌宴、赏赐、争宠、请安、宫宴、寿宴、药膳、贡品、妃嫔互相设局. Product role: final proof, secret weapon, status signal, or care object.
- **古装反差带货**: 古代人物认真使用现代商品, such as delivery, phone order, app tool, appliance, beauty product, or travel item. Product role: impossible shortcut that breaks the old-world limit.
- **轻功 / 武侠带货**: everyone relies on speed, messengers, martial ability, or manual labor; the product quietly beats the expected heroic method. Product role: faster-than-lightwork solution, hidden resource, or smarter nonviolent win.
- **重生古装带货**: 上一世 because the protagonist lacked or chose the wrong product, they lost face, status, money, love, or survival. This life they use the modern product early and reverse the same scene.

Weighting rules:

- Make the modern product higher priority than generic ancient atmosphere. Do not let the video become only a pretty costume drama.
- Bind modern artifacts explicitly: `现代手机`, `产品App页面`, `订单状态`, `配送袋`, `外卖骑手`, `商品包装`, `完成页`, or other product-specific proof.
- Stage at least one strong 古今同框 proof shot, such as `清宫宫门 + 外卖骑手 + 密封配送袋`, `旗装嫔妃 + 手机下单页`, `古代宴桌 + 现代商品包装`, or `皇上/掌门/王爷认真看订单完成页`.
- The reversal must depend on the modern product. If removing the modern object still lets the protagonist win, the product weight is too low.
- Do not play it as pure parody. The contrast is the hook; the public reversal is the爽点; the product is the evidence.

Hook patterns:

- "妹妹掌宴，竟连皇上爱喝的酒都备不齐？"
- "宫门都落锁了，我看你拿什么补。"
- "上一世，我轻功追了一夜，还是晚了。这一世，我不追了。"
- "全府都等着看她出丑，直到宫门外来了一个穿配送服的人。"

15s structure:

```
0-3s: 古装权力场公开羞辱、上一世失败闪回、或结果先行的异常画面；对手抬高压力, insisting old-world rules make the protagonist doomed
3-7s: 主角不慌，视线、手部或站位显示她已经有隐藏选择；主角认真拿出现代产品 and uses it with a clear screen/package/action proof
7-12s [PRODUCT]: 古今同框 proof shot; modern product arrives, works, or produces evidence
12-15s: ancient witness reaction + antagonist loses face + conversion line tied to the modern product
```

### Backup Modes Only

Tutorial, expert backing, trend montage, and pure product demo are fallback formats, not the default for this skill. Use them only when the product cannot credibly create relationship pressure, public misjudgment, or visible reversal. If using a backup mode, still add at least one human pressure line before product explanation.

## Three-Second Hook Rules

The first 3 seconds must be melodramatic enough to stop the scroll. Start with a dog-blood relationship event, public pressure, or abnormal state, not a product claim. The opening should make viewers ask "what happened?" or "how will they fight back?" before they know what is being sold.

Choose one:

- **Conflict already happening**: accusation, deadline, rejection, public embarrassment.
- **Result before cause**: huge output, shocked reaction, sold-out result, impossible transformation.
- **Wrong judgment**: viewers think the protagonist is lazy, broke, cheating, incapable, weird, arrogant.
- **Relationship rupture**: someone important appears disappointed, suspicious, cold, or dismissive.
- **Hidden cause**: the protagonist behaves strangely, and the audience wants to know why.
- **Dog-blood provocation**: love rival, ex, mother-in-law, boss, classmate, client, spouse, or friend says the most hurtful wrong judgment out loud.
- **Public exposure**: the protagonist is exposed, compared, mocked, or forced to prove themselves in front of people who matter.
- **Rebirth flashback (重生开局)**: black-and-white flashback of past-life failure + "上一世，[具体惨痛]" voiceover, then cut to color present + "这一世，[短决心]". The audience instantly enters preemptive-revenge mode.

The opening line should be specific and sharp enough to create instant emotional debt:

- "你穿成这样，是怕别人看出来吗？"
- "十分钟做不出来，你今天就别干了。"
- "我儿子娶你，不是让你乱花钱的。"
- "她这种人，也配站在合照中间？"
- "你是不是又背着我买没用的东西？"

Avoid hooks that only say "this product is useful". They lack story pressure. Also avoid empty shock that the product cannot pay off. The dog-blood event must connect to the exact product proof later.

## Story Construction

Use this compact story order for 15-60 second videos:

1. **0-3s Dog-blood visual hook + relationship pressure**: one pressure image, accusation, humiliation, betrayal hint, public comparison, abnormal behavior, or impossible result that immediately signals the short-drama type. Make clear who has power over whom, who is misreading whom, and what the protagonist risks losing. No product intro unless the product is already part of the public accusation.
2. **3-7s Escalation + hidden choice/product activation**: let the antagonist raise stakes while the protagonist's gaze, hand, or posture shows a hidden choice starting. If the product needs operation, show the first clear product action here.
3. **7-12s Product proof**: show the product as the protagonist's move: evidence, secret weapon, caring gift, status signal, wrong-choice correction, or reveal key. The product must enable a result the protagonist could not credibly get otherwise.
4. **12-15s Genre payoff + product-name line**: face-slap, protection, reconciliation, professional recognition, hidden identity reveal, family recognition, comedy release, or emotional fulfillment, ending with a spoken product-name conversion line.

For 15-60 second videos, expand the same order rather than returning to equal time chunks: hook fast, pressure clear, proof longest, payoff clean.

For very short 10-15s videos, compress to:

**hook -> pressure -> product proof -> reaction -> value line**

When turning a 15-second route into a `video_generation_prompt`, express this order as 3-4 timestamped shots, not 5-6 micro-beats. Combine hook + pressure into the first shot, product choice + activation into the second, proof into the third, and payoff + product-name line into the fourth.

For a series, keep the same product meaning but rotate the pressure scene:

- Episode 1 proves the core use case.
- Episode 2 proves the product under social comparison.
- Episode 3 proves the product under time pressure or public pressure.
- Episode 4 proves relationship recognition, such as being gifted, defended, or understood.
- Episode 5 proves repeat purchase or recommendation, such as another character asking for the link.

Do not make each episode a random new benefit. The viewer should feel like they are watching different stories that all teach the same product truth.

## Camera, Editing, And Sound

Treat the audiovisual plan as part of the idea, not a later production note.

- **Public Reversal**: tight close-ups, oppressive angles for the antagonist, protagonist framed smaller before reversal, silence before proof, impact sound on reveal, reaction close-up after proof.
- **Relationship Recognition**: use softer two-shot framing after the reveal, contrast earlier distance/coldness with later care or protection, hold the product long enough to read as emotional evidence.
- **Deadline / Competence Win**: fast escalation, clock or countdown only if it raises pressure, quick proof shots, then slow down for the delivered result and witness reaction.
- **Misunderstanding Reveal**: keep pre-reveal shots audience-visible only; use sound bridges and deadpan reaction cuts; reveal late enough to recontextualize earlier behavior.
- **Wrong-Choice Correction**: show the old mistake briefly, mirror the same scene with the smarter choice, and make the contrast visible without long explanation.

Pacing rule:

**escalation fast, reveal slow enough to land, product explanation short.**

Sound rule:

Use sound as punctuation: impact for accusation, brief silence before reveal, whoosh for transition, music lift for payoff, and one clean hit for "proof appears".

## Product Integration Rules

- Make the product a **key**: it unlocks the conflict, fantasy, shortcut, or status reversal.
- Show proof before explanation whenever possible.
- Avoid early feature dumping. Viewers should first care about the problem or desire.
- Match the product to the short-drama genre's payoff. A beauty product in 女频逆袭 should prove visible transformation or confidence; a tool in 职场爽剧 should produce a deadline result; a home/health product in 家庭伦理 should make care or labor visible; a giftable product in 甜宠 should prove the other person noticed a hidden need.
- If the product is a health, supplement, finance, or other claim-sensitive category, make it a believable routine choice, care signal, or problem-fit decision. Do not make it magically solve body, medical, wealth, or life outcomes.
- If the product is software, show human pressure before screen recording. Screen UI alone rarely carries drama.
- If the product is physical, design the shot so the benefit is visible: surface, body area, texture, stain, output, reaction, or before/after.
- If the product is a service or course, make the proof a believable changed behavior, result, saved trouble, or expert process instead of an abstract promise.
- If the product is food, beauty, fashion, home, or lifestyle, make the proof sensory or socially observable: taste reaction, skin/clothing/home before-after, confidence shift, guest reaction, daily friction disappearing.
- For wine and banquet beverage stories, make the protagonist's styling match the product's social role. If the product proves taste, hospitality, or status under public judgment, the main character should look prepared for the occasion, not casually plain; the outfit can echo the wine color or dinner setting while keeping the bottle/glass as the main visual proof.
- Keep the final value line concrete: "10 minutes clean a greasy stove" is stronger than "deep cleaning power"; "one person can finish three episodes in an afternoon" is stronger than "improve productivity".

## Creative Micro-Drama Expansion

Do not default to modern family, office, roommate, or daily-life skits unless the product clearly needs realism for trust, safety, or practical demonstration. When the user asks for 创意, 微剧情, 爽感, 记忆点, 体裁更丰富, or says the first draft feels too plain, switch to the **creative micro-drama lane** by default.

Before scripting, choose a **creative container** and a **visual reference**. The container controls the story world; the reference controls the image, lens, lighting, costume, pacing, and mood. Product proof stays higher priority than the container. If the container makes the product less believable or hides the product proof, choose a sharper container.

Useful creative containers:

- **古装错位**: 清宫宫斗, 镖局, 武侠, 民国豪门, 古代审判, modern product as serious plot weapon.
- **重生爽剧**: 上一世失败, 这一世提前用 product choice to reverse the same public loss.
- **悬疑审判**: 电梯密室, 婚礼审判席, 身份审查, 证物反转, courtroom-like proof.
- **综艺挑战**: 限时挑战, 观察室, 恋综, 家政比赛, 试衣间审判, live reaction cuts.
- **轻科幻 / 末日**: 未来闸机, 冰封城市, 避难所, AI审核, red-alert pressure.
- **超现实日常**: 时间循环, 镜子说话, 物品拟人, 梦境回放, magical-realism proof.
- **霸总 / 名媛 / 豪门**: 舞会, 签约局, 家族饭桌, public status humiliation.

### Avoid Safe Skit Drift

In batch generation, if multiple outputs start with similar scenes such as 家庭餐桌, 办公室会议, 闺蜜试衣, 婆婆质疑, stop and diversify the dramatic container. Do not repeat the same creative container more than twice unless the user asks for a specific genre.

For each product, ask:

- Can this become 古装错位?
- Can this become 重生?
- Can this become 悬疑审判?
- Can this become 综艺挑战 or 恋综观察室?
- Can this become 末日, 未来, or 超现实设定?

Keep the realistic version only when the creative container weakens the product proof, credibility, or conversion.
