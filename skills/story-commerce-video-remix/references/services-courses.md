# Services / Courses / Local Business

Use for courses, coaching, consulting, repair, local stores, salons, gyms, clinics where allowed, agencies, photography, travel services, and other non-tangible offers.

## Buying Psychology

- Users buy trust, reduced uncertainty, saved mistakes, expert guidance, visible progress, convenience, and "someone handles it for me".
- The proof cannot be only a promise. Show a changed behavior, finished deliverable, solved situation, expert process, social reaction, or measurable result.
- The service provider can be a character, but avoid turning the video into a self-introduction too early.

## Best Formulas

- **Professional Nanny Tutorial**: show the process in a way that lowers fear and makes next steps clear.
- **Expert Backing**: prove standards, diagnosis, workflow, quality control, and why amateurs fail.
- **Status Reversal**: someone thought help was unnecessary; after a crisis, the service proves its value.
- **Dream Fulfillment**: course/service helps the user reach a role identity: confident speaker, better parent, stronger body, camera-ready bride, organized business owner.

## Proof Forms

- Before/after work sample, client reaction, solved crisis, checklist/process, measured improvement, fewer mistakes, time saved, visible confidence.
- Local business: arrival experience, staff handling, result reveal, customer returning with someone else.
- Course: first failed attempt, guided correction, second attempt result, learner reaction.

## Hook Patterns

- "她花了三个月自学，第一步就错了。"
- "客户进门第一句话：你们能不能今天救回来？"
- "他本来觉得没必要请人，直到现场出事。"
- "这不是天赋问题，是没人告诉你顺序。"

## Example Routes

- Course: a beginner repeatedly fails a visible task; the instructor changes one method; the second attempt succeeds on camera.
- Repair service: a family tries three DIY fixes before a deadline; the expert shows one diagnostic step that explains the real issue.
- Local salon: a customer arrives after a bad self-styling attempt; the reveal is not just beauty but regained confidence before an important event.

## Risks

- Avoid fake guaranteed results or exaggerated earnings.
- Do not over-index on credentials; dramatize the problem first.
- For regulated categories like medical, finance, legal, or health, keep claims compliant and avoid giving personalized advice.
