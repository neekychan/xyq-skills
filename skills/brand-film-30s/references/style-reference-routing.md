# Style Reference Routing for Brand Films

Use this reference to choose a visual style for 30-second brand films and Seedance 2.5 prompts. The style reference is mandatory, but the style library is open.

## Selection Rule

Choose style by brand task:

**brand task -> audience feeling -> category tension -> reference mode -> style keywords -> borrowed dimensions**

Do not start from a favorite film style. Start from what the brand needs to become in the viewer's mind.

For Seedance 2.5, weak style labels such as `cinematic`, `premium`, `moody`, `urban`, `clean`, `documentary`, or `noir` are not enough. Use a **strong style anchor** that can immediately change the generated image:

**recognizable film / advertising genre -> camera grammar -> lighting system -> color palette -> texture -> motion rule -> sound motif**

## Concrete Style Translation Rule

Every style choice must become production craft, not a label. Before using any style reference in a route or prompt, translate it into:

- **Camera language**: shot distance, lens behavior, camera height, movement, framing rule, foreground/background relationship, and editing rhythm.
- **Visual style / art direction**: location world, set logic, props, wardrobe, body language, production design, texture, atmosphere, and recurring visual code.
- **Color and material system**: palette, contrast, saturation, color temperature, surface behavior, fabric/skin/glass/metal/food/vehicle highlights, and brand-coded accents.
- **Sound design**: music direction, ambience, close-mic Foley, silence, rhythm, sonic motif, voice texture, impact sounds, or environmental cues.

A style is ready only when the route can answer: what does the camera repeatedly do, what visual world does the audience recognize, what color/material rule owns the image, and what sound makes the brand film memorable?

Good Seedance style anchors feel like production directions, not mood words. They should name a typical filmic world or advertising lineage, then translate it into visible rules:

- `Wong Kar-wai-style rainy neon romance`: compressed long lens, red/green neon spill, wet glass reflections, step-print feeling, cigarette-smoke haze, lonely close-ups, jukebox-like music pulse.
- `Shunji Iwai-style lyrical youth memory`: soft backlight, overexposed highlights, pale blue/white/green palette, handheld close observation, wind/curtain/grass movement, handwritten or diary-like fragments, piano or airy ambient music.
- `Denis Villeneuve-style monumental sci-fi`: vast negative space, brutalist architecture, dust haze, amber/teal low-key light, slow axial push-in, tiny human against huge machinery, sub-bass drones.
- `David Fincher-style cold corporate thriller`: controlled camera, symmetrical offices, green-gray shadows, practical monitor glow, precise slow dolly, low contrast faces, quiet mechanical sound.
- `Christopher Nolan-style practical-action spectacle`: IMAX-scale wide shots, real physics, high-contrast daylight, cross-cutting pressure, heavy vehicle/object motion, ticking percussion.
- `Wes Anderson-style symmetrical storybook tableau`: centered locked frames, pastel blocking, flat lateral movement, miniature-like set design, deadpan performance, chapter-card rhythm.
- `A24-style surreal suburbia`: ordinary domestic spaces, slightly wrong props, fixed wide frames, cold daylight, awkward silence, restrained performance, one uncanny visual rule.
- `Tony Scott / 1990s action-thriller advertising`: hard sun flare, telephoto compression, whip pan, orange-blue contrast, heat shimmer, aggressive cuts, metallic percussion.
- `Ridley Scott-style smoky industrial epic`: backlit smoke, wet metal, shafts of light, scale architecture, silhouettes, slow reveal, low mechanical drones.
- `Wong Kar-wai / 2046 luxury night`: saturated red-gold-green palette, mirrors and glass layers, close faces cut by foreground objects, slow motion fragments, romantic melancholy.
- `Terrence Malick-style natural-light memory film`: golden-hour backlight, handheld floating camera, grass/water/skin highlights, whispered human presence, elliptical cuts.
- `German Expressionist shadow theatre`: distorted shadows, hard diagonal light, high-contrast set geometry, theatrical silhouettes, uneasy movement.
- `Italian giallo / fashion horror color`: saturated red/blue/green gels, glossy black surfaces, macro objects, gloved hands, sharp zooms, synth pulse.
- `1980s VHS night-drive`: tape noise, halation, dashboard glow, analog color bleed, handheld passenger-seat camera, muffled radio, sodium-vapor streetlight.
- `Y2K glossy music-video commercial`: chrome, glass, fisheye/wide lens, flash cuts, strobe, high-saturation cyan/magenta, dance-like product motion.
- `Luxury fragrance myth film`: sculptural body/product, gold skin light, slow dolly, silk/metal/water texture, palace/desert/ocean scale, ritual gestures, operatic sound.
- `High-fashion flash photography campaign`: direct flash, hard shadows, cut-off faces, aggressive posing, white wall or backstage set, jump cuts, shutter sound.
- `Mugler-like avant-garde fashion body spectacle`: engineered silhouette, corset/body-architecture logic, glossy black surfaces, metallic skin highlights, theatrical runway energy, direct gaze, sharp hip/waist/shoulder geometry, bass-heavy club or runway sound.
- `Aesthetic-core social style world`: choose one core and translate it into wardrobe, location, color, props, body language, sound, and platform behavior instead of using the core name as a label.
- `Brutalist Nordic minimalism`: concrete/steel/wood, cold sky light, sparse composition, long silences, clean side light, restrained human movement.
- `Neo-noir rain city`: wet asphalt, sodium-vapor highlights, venetian blinds or windshield shadows, long lens bokeh, low-key faces, jazz/synth low pulse.
- `Cold-war retro-futurist lab`: cream consoles, rounded instruments, hard top light, symmetrical frames, analog meters, teal/orange practical bulbs, synth hum.
- `Stop-motion handmade tabletop`: tactile paper/fabric/clay texture, locked camera, tiny object choreography, visible craft marks, clicky Foley.
- `Shounen anime opening`: speed lines, ink impact frames, dynamic low angles, character silhouette reveal, cut-in eyes/hands, rhythmic title-card energy.
- `Prestige sports anthem`: low-angle hero bodies, sweat and breath, stadium backlight, fast match cuts, crowd pulse, percussion build.

Use named directors, films, or advertising lineages as **style shorthand only**. The prompt must borrow production dimensions, not protected characters, exact scenes, exact dialogue, franchise symbols, or celebrity likenesses.

Style references are not limited to classic cinema. They may come from auteur film aesthetics, fashion-house visual languages, runway/campaign systems, music-video eras, platform-native formats, aesthetic cores, subcultures, game worlds, interface cultures, editorial photography, or motion-design languages. When using a brand- or designer-like reference such as Mugler-like body spectacle, borrow the production mechanism only: silhouette engineering, body architecture, runway theatricality, lighting, camera, material, and sound. Do not copy protected garments, logos, campaign scenes, celebrity looks, or another brand's exact signature.

## Seedance 2.5 Style Anchor Rules

- Prefer one strong anchor over a stack of mild references. `Wong Kar-wai rainy neon romance` is stronger than `cinematic, emotional, urban, premium`.
- Put the style anchor at the start of `风格与视觉参考`, then immediately spell out camera, lighting, color, texture, motion, and sound.
- Treat every style anchor as a craft checklist: camera language, visual style / art direction, color and material system, lighting behavior, editing rhythm, and sound motif must all be present before the prompt is final.
- Choose anchors that fit the brand's category tension. Auto safety can use `David Fincher-style cold thriller`, `neo-noir rain city`, `Nolan practical-action spectacle`, `Brutalist Nordic minimalism`, or `1980s VHS night-drive`; luxury beauty can use `luxury fragrance myth`, `giallo fashion color`, or `high-fashion flash photography`.
- Use genre intensity responsibly. Strong style should make the product truth clearer, not turn every brand into horror, cyberpunk, or action.
- If using a protected film/director reference, convert it into generic production language in the final prompt when brand safety matters: `rainy neon romance with compressed long lenses` instead of relying only on a name.

## Scale Calibration For Grand Style Anchors

Some style anchors are powerful but easily become too grand for a 30-second generated brand film: `luxury fragrance myth`, `Denis Villeneuve-style monumental sci-fi`, `epic brand anthem`, `post-apocalyptic survival`, `atomic punk`, `large-scale crowd film`, `natural-force imagery`, `史诗尺度`, `神话感`, `未来感`, and `宏大叙事`.

When using any grand style anchor, ground it with a **scale ladder** before writing the final prompt:

1. **Human-scale proof**: one face, hand, body action, object, or product behavior that can be filmed close enough to understand.
2. **Material-scale world**: one surface, prop, texture, light source, reflection, dust, smoke, water, fabric, metal, paper, or screen behavior that makes the style visible.
3. **Spatial-scale image**: one wide frame, architectural depth, empty negative space, crowd edge, landscape, or large object that gives scale.
4. **Brand-scale consequence**: how the product or brand changes the human action, material behavior, or spatial image before the lockup.

Do not let the style live only in words such as `epic`, `mythic`, `monumental`, `luxury`, `future`, `survival`, or `cinematic`. In the final prompt, convert them into concrete production devices:

- `epic` -> low-angle body against a real stadium tunnel, wind hitting fabric, percussion synced to footsteps, one wide proof frame.
- `mythic` -> ritual hand gesture, gold edge light on product, silk / stone / water texture, slow axial push, operatic breath or single choir note.
- `monumental sci-fi` -> tiny subject in front of one vast machine or brutalist wall, dust haze in side light, slow axial camera, sub-bass drone, product as the only active light source.
- `post-apocalyptic` -> repaired object, dust, taped metal, harsh sun, hand testing durability, functional costume, silence plus wind.
- `atomic punk` -> cream console, analog meter, hard top light, symmetrical frame, rounded metal instrument, synth hum, one clear product-triggered action.

For 30-second outputs, prefer one iconic scale image plus one close proof action. Large worlds need fewer ideas, not more scenery.

## Surreal Visual Rule

Use this rule when the route calls for surreal, uncanny, dreamlike, impossible, mythic transformation, heightened visual logic, or `超现实`. Surrealism should make the brand logic more visible, not make the film merely stranger.

Surreal is not a synonym for future, premium, cinematic, dreamy, VFX-heavy, or strange. It means:

**realistic base + one impossible rule + visible consequence + brand causality**

Before writing the final prompt, define:

- **Realistic base**: the ordinary photographed world before the rule changes, such as office, street, kitchen, runway, car interior, bedroom, store, studio, road, or product table.
- **Impossible rule**: one physical, social, spatial, material, time, gravity, media, or reflection rule that changes.
- **Trigger**: the product, brand asset, gesture, sound, light, object, line, or human decision that activates the rule.
- **Visible consequence**: what changes in space, body, material, scale, time, reflection, weather, screen, sound, or social behavior.
- **Brand causality**: why only this brand or product can trigger, explain, resolve, measure, protect, or witness the impossible rule.
- **Camera proof**: the camera move or locked frame that proves the impossible event has scale and physical presence.
- **Restraint**: one surreal rule per 30-second film. Do not stack unrelated impossible effects.

Common surreal rule types:

| Rule type | What changes | Useful for |
|---|---|---|
| **Spatial rule** | rooms stretch, roads fold, city lines become paths, interiors become corridors, architecture responds to product action | AI, platforms, travel, auto, home, fashion, services |
| **Scale rule** | product becomes architectural, person enters product interior, small object controls a city-scale system | luxury, tech, jewelry, auto, packaging, platforms |
| **Material rule** | fabric behaves like water, glass behaves like skin, metal flows, paper becomes road, light becomes solid | beauty, fashion, food, alcohol, home, consumer electronics |
| **Time rule** | world freezes, loops, reverses, delays, or only product-linked action moves normally | productivity, sport, finance, insurance, repair, memory brands |
| **Gravity / physics rule** | bodies, products, liquids, clothing, or architecture briefly ignore gravity while retaining weight and shadow | sport, fashion, beverage, auto, entertainment, youth brands |
| **Media rule** | screens, photos, packaging, mirrors, lenses, posters, or projections enter reality, or reality is compressed into media | social platforms, creator tools, fashion, beauty, entertainment, AI |
| **Social rule** | invisible pressure becomes visible: gaze turns into spotlights, reviews become stickers, deadlines become shadows, status becomes distance | workplace, B2B, finance, education, beauty, fashion, lifestyle |

Prompt formula:

```text
现实基底：{ordinary photographed world}
超现实规则：{one impossible rule}
触发机制：{product / brand asset / gesture / sound / light}
视觉后果：{what physically changes}
品牌因果：{why this brand or product matters}
镜头验证：{camera move or locked proof frame}
光影与材质：{how the impossible event touches light, shadow, reflection, surface, air, or sound}
生成约束：one surreal rule only, keep product/logo/human identity readable, no random VFX stack
```

In final Seedance prompts, embed the surreal rule into `风格与视觉参考`, `场景`, and the timed beats. Do not output `surreal` or `超现实` as a standalone mood label.

## Style Reference Groups

Use common film, advertising, and production language for style reference groups. In final outputs, prefer a specific film lineage, advertising lineage, director-like visual school, or production description over an internal label.

### Observational Life / Naturalistic Drama

Use when the brand needs warmth, trust, everyday truth, or gentle premium feeling.

Reference modes:

- Japanese life cinema
- family naturalism
- observational documentary
- European quiet drama

Keywords:

- `自然光`, `空气感`, `低声对话`, `生活褶皱`, `手部细节`, `环境声`, `克制情绪`, `留白构图`

Good for:

- food, home, family services, health-adjacent, beauty care, travel memory, local lifestyle.

Avoid:

- making the film too slow for 30 seconds; keep one clear emotional movement.

### Urban Romance and Soft Drama

Use when the brand specifically needs approachable desirability, intimacy, modern lifestyle, or polished everyday aspiration. Treat this as a lifestyle / relationship / urban service mode, not a default premium brand-film reference. For luxury, fragrance, streetwear, fashion drops, youth culture, or image-led brand films, usually choose a more authored style such as luxury ritual, indie-film fragments, fashion movement, documentary realism, analog texture, surrealism, or a stronger genre world.

Reference modes:

- Korean drama visual grammar
- urban romance
- hotel/lobby/commute emotional worlds
- soft-focus city films

Keywords:

- `都市柔光`, `浅景深特写`, `玻璃反射`, `干净室内`, `通勤精致`, `雨后街道`, `轻情绪`, `柔和肤色`

Good for:

- beauty, fashion, jewelry, coffee, travel, mobile devices, lifestyle services.

Avoid:

- generic pretty people with no category tension.
- using this as the default for luxury, fragrance, streetwear, trend, or global brand-image films; it can feel too normal, too television-like, and insufficiently distinctive.

### Documentary Realism

Use when the brand needs credibility, human truth, social relevance, or proof.

Reference modes:

- observational documentary
- sports training documentary
- backstage realism
- street-level testimony

Keywords:

- `手持纪实`, `自然光`, `非表演感`, `真实皮肤纹理`, `现场杂音`, `呼吸感镜头`, `后台拥挤`, `不完美细节`

Good for:

- sport, beauty confidence, social impact, B2B real work, craft, local services.

Avoid:

- over-polishing the image until it loses truth.

### Lyrical Youth Memory / Iwai-Like Poetic Realism

Use when the brand needs fragile emotion, youth memory, first love, friendship, distance, nostalgia, quiet longing, or a soft but specific human world. This style is useful for beauty, fashion, beverages, music platforms, travel memory, stationery, cameras, local lifestyle, campus stories, and any brand that wants poetic sensitivity rather than a loud manifesto.

Do not use this style as a vague synonym for `日系清新`. Its recognizable production code is: low saturation, pale blue-white-green color, high-key natural window light, visibly overexposed white curtains / paper / sky, shallow focus, handheld floating camera, partial body framing, white-light dissolves, gentle jump cuts, film grain, dust in light, and wind moving curtains, paper, grass, or hair.

Reference modes:

- Shunji Iwai-style lyrical youth cinema
- Japanese 1990s youth memory film
- diary-like coming-of-age fragments
- music-video-influenced soft realism

Keywords:

- `低饱和`, `白色过曝`, `淡蓝白绿色调`, `柔软逆光`, `浅景深`, `手持漂浮`, `局部身体构图`, `白光溶接`, `风吹窗帘`, `钢琴与环境声`

Craft rules:

- **Camera language**: handheld or lightly floating camera, shallow depth of field, close faces and hands, partial bodies, foreground occlusion by curtains / glass / paper, edge-of-frame subjects, long pauses, soft focus, overexposed windows, white-light dissolves, gentle jump cuts, fragments that feel remembered rather than fully explained.
- **Visual style / art direction**: school corridor, bedroom, train platform, riverside, field, small town street, old photo, handwritten note, cassette, CD, phone message, curtain, glass, rain, wind, paper, dust in window light, blank pages, pale sky.
- **Color and material system**: low saturation, pale blue, white, washed green, gray-beige, soft skin, paper texture, translucent curtains, natural fabric, slight film grain, halation around highlights, white areas intentionally clipped or nearly clipped.
- **Lighting behavior**: natural window light from side-back direction, strong backlight through white curtains, overexposed sky / paper / fabric, clean low-contrast shadows on faces, soft rim on hair and fingers, exposure drift that makes the image feel like memory rather than commercial polish.
- **Sound design**: sparse piano motif, room tone, wind, distant train, page turning, breath, small object sounds, quiet keyboard or pencil sounds when relevant, large areas of silence after an emotional action.

Good for:

- brands that need emotional delicacy, youth memory, music, letter/message motifs, gentle product rituals, nostalgic but modern tone.

Avoid:

- using softness as generic prettiness; the scene still needs a visible desire, missed connection, message, object, or decision.
- forgetting the low-saturation and overexposure system; without clipped whites, shallow focus, partial framing, and quiet environmental sound, the result will look like ordinary lifestyle advertising rather than Iwai-like youth memory.

### Luxury Fashion / Fragrance Film

Use when the brand needs ritual, desire, rarity, beauty, or symbolic elevation.

Reference modes:

- luxury fragrance films
- fashion myth
- sculpture/body cinema
- palace/desert/ocean ritual imagery

Keywords:

- `金色皮肤光`, `雕塑化身体`, `仪式动作`, `丝绸与金属`, `宫殿尺度`, `慢速推轨`, `水面反光`, `神话感`

Craft rules:

- **Camera language**: slow axial push, profile close-up, macro product edge, hand ritual, sculptural body silhouette, one wide architectural frame, long hold before the lockup.
- **Visual style / art direction**: palace stair, black reflective table, water surface, silk curtain, stone wall, desert / ocean / empty gallery only when the brand needs scale; keep one ritual object or product as the center.
- **Color and material system**: gold skin light, black negative space, pearl white, deep shadow, silk, stone, water, glass, polished metal, product edge highlight.
- **Lighting behavior**: narrow side-back gold light on skin / bottle / device edge, low fill, controlled shadow, specular reflection on glass or metal, final logo supported by dark negative space.
- **Sound design**: sparse operatic breath, single choir note, silk / glass / water Foley, low sub tone, silence before product reveal, refined sonic logo.

Good for:

- fragrance, beauty, jewelry, premium beverages, fashion, luxury travel, high-end devices.

Avoid:

- empty beauty with no brand idea.

### Avant-Garde Fashion Body / Mugler-Like Spectacle

Use when the brand needs fashion-forward power, engineered silhouette, body confidence, nightlife, provocation, performance, sensuality, or a futuristic runway-world proposition. This is most useful for fashion, fragrance, beauty, eyewear, jewelry, nightlife, music, premium fitness, and youth luxury.

Reference modes:

- Mugler-like avant-garde fashion body spectacle
- engineered silhouette fashion film
- runway-theatre body architecture
- glossy club/future glamour campaign

Keywords:

- `身体建筑`, `束腰轮廓`, `锐利肩线`, `高光黑色乳胶`, `金属皮肤光`, `直视镜头`, `闪光灯定格`, `低频秀场节拍`

Craft rules:

- **Camera language**: low-angle body close-ups, sharp side profiles, direct-to-camera gaze, runway tracking, flash-photo freeze frames, orbit around silhouette, hard cut from body detail to full-body architecture.
- **Visual style / art direction**: glossy black floor, white studio void, backstage mirror bulbs, club runway, chrome railings, sculptural stairs, theatrical haze, engineered garment shapes, powerful stance.
- **Color and material system**: black, chrome, blood red, electric blue, skin highlight, latex/leather/metal/glass shine, hard specular reflections, high contrast, clean silhouette edges.
- **Sound design**: runway footfall, camera shutter, bass hits, latex/fabric friction, crowd breath, club pulse, sudden silence before a silhouette reveal.

Good for:

- brands that need a provocative fashion world, body-as-architecture, strong silhouette memory, or a premium nightlife/future glamour feeling.

Avoid:

- copying exact Mugler garments, logos, celebrity runway looks, or making provocation replace the product/brand role.

### Music and Dance Film

Use when the brand needs youth, memorability, participation, social spread, or body-language ownership.

Reference modes:

- music video
- dance film
- fashion movement campaign
- group choreography

Keywords:

- `群像调度`, `节拍剪辑`, `强色块`, `可模仿动作`, `身体记忆`, `快速变装`, `街区能量`, `舞台灯`

Good for:

- fashion, denim, sneakers, snacks, beverages, creator tools, youth platforms.

Avoid:

- choreography that does not reveal the product or brand role.

### Aesthetic-Core / Subculture Style Worlds

Use when the brand needs to enter a current audience identity, internet-born taste world, fashion tribe, lifestyle code, or social platform language. A `core` is not a finished style by itself; it is a shorthand for wardrobe, props, location, color, posture, sound, platform behavior, and social meaning.

Reference modes:

- gorpcore / outdoor utility world
- balletcore / soft discipline and rehearsal world
- blokecore / football terrace and pub-street uniform
- office siren / corporate Y2K sensuality
- coquette / ribboned softness and hyper-feminine detail
- cottagecore / pastoral handmade nostalgia
- cybercore / digital body and interface culture
- weirdcore / uncanny internet image logic
- kidcore / toy-color nostalgia
- quiet luxury / restrained material status
- dark academia / library ritual and intellectual mood

Keywords:

- `core转译`, `亚文化道具`, `平台原生姿态`, `服装编码`, `场景身份`, `色彩纪律`, `声音标识`, `社群识别`

Craft rules:

- **Camera language**: choose the camera behavior that belongs to the core, such as phone POV, flash diary, locked editorial frame, handheld street fragment, rehearsal mirror view, outdoor action cam, desktop screen capture, or surveillance-like image.
- **Visual style / art direction**: define the core through 3-5 visible anchors: location, wardrobe piece, object, body posture, social setting, and one recurring motif.
- **Color and material system**: every core needs a palette and material rule, such as moss/nylon/rain shell for gorpcore, pale pink/ribbon/satin for balletcore, gray office glass/black pencil skirt/metal glasses for office siren, or brown leather/paper/wood for dark academia.
- **Sound design**: use a matching sonic code, such as hiking zipper and wind, ballet shoe floor scrape, football chant, keyboard and heel clicks, ribbon/fabric rustle, bird/wind/paper ambience, modem/glitch texture, or library silence.

Good for:

- fashion, beauty, sneakers, outdoor, music, creator platforms, youth beverages, social apps, travel, campus, lifestyle, and brands that need audience identity rather than broad mainstream polish.

Avoid:

- dropping a core name without visible proof; each core must become camera, wardrobe, location, color, material, gesture, and sound.

### Physical Comedy / Deadpan Comedy

Use when the brand needs approachability, memorability, or a clever demonstration of friction.

Reference modes:

- Jacques Tati-like spatial humor
- deadpan office comedy
- absurd everyday escalation
- silent visual gag

Keywords:

- `固定机位`, `空间调度`, `冷幽默`, `动作错位`, `重复节奏`, `现代建筑`, `尴尬停顿`, `反差表演`

Good for:

- service platforms, productivity tools, travel, insurance, retail, food delivery.

Avoid:

- jokes that do not connect back to the product function.

### Surrealism / Magical Realism

Use when the brand needs a memorable twist, cultural edge, or a way to visualize an invisible problem.

Reference modes:

- A24-like slight uncanniness
- surreal suburbia
- magical realist everyday

Keywords:

- `普通生活场景`, `轻微异样`, `固定镜头`, `安静不安`, `超现实小偏差`, `冷色日光`, `怪诞道具`, `克制表演`

Good for:

- tech privacy, AI, finance anxiety, service friction, personal care taboos.

Avoid:

- weirdness for its own sake.

### Noir and Thriller

Use when the brand needs tension, risk, protection, secrecy, or control.

Reference modes:

- noir city rain
- spy thriller
- cold-war control room
- corporate thriller

Keywords:

- `黑色电影雨夜`, `湿地反光`, `低调光`, `百叶窗阴影`, `监控视角`, `慢速推轨`, `烟雾层次`, `低频压迫`

Good for:

### Retro-Futurism / Atomic Age Design

Use when the brand needs playful future, nostalgic technology, or designed artificiality.

Reference modes:

- atomic punk
- retro-futurism
- cold-war lab
- space-age domestic design

Keywords:

- `原子朋克`, `奶油色控制台`, `圆角金属仪器`, `复古未来主义`, `硬边顶光`, `粉蓝撞色`, `对称构图`, `合成器氛围`

Good for:

- consumer tech, AI, appliances, creator tools, playful brands.

Avoid:

- making serious brands look gimmicky.

### Epic Brand Anthem

Use when the brand needs belief, courage, movement, or category leadership.

Reference modes:

- sports anthem
- cinematic manifesto
- large-scale crowd film
- natural-force imagery

Keywords:

- `大远景`, `低频鼓点`, `逆光剪影`, `群像推进`, `风暴天气`, `强旁白`, `身体极限`, `史诗尺度`

Craft rules:

- **Camera language**: start from one specific human pressure, then expand to two or three evidence scenes; use low-angle body frames, match cuts on repeated movement, one wide scale frame, and a clean final proof frame.
- **Visual style / art direction**: stadium tunnel, street corner, workshop, field, public square, storm edge, dawn road, or workplace under pressure; every location needs a visible task or risk.
- **Color and material system**: high-contrast backlight, sweat, rain, dust, fabric movement, asphalt, chalk, metal, skin highlights; avoid generic glossy crowd footage.
- **Lighting behavior**: backlight or side light makes bodies readable as silhouettes, then a warmer or clearer proof light appears when the brand consequence lands.
- **Sound design**: breath, footsteps, crowd edge, low percussion, silence before the final line, sonic logo after the brand lockup.

Good for:

- sport, auto, public campaigns, brand launches, legacy brand renewal.

Avoid:

- empty slogans without a concrete human pressure.
- replacing evidence scenes with only large crowd, mountains, storms, or voiceover.

### Post-Apocalyptic and Survival Worlds

Use only when scarcity, resilience, environmental stress, repair, or survival directly serves the category tension.

Reference modes:

- post-apocalyptic road worlds
- survival cinema
- desert/wasteland futures

Keywords:

- `末日废土`, `沙尘空气`, `低饱和暖黄`, `破损金属`, `远景孤独感`, `手持追踪`, `功能性服装`, `生存质感`

Craft rules:

- **Camera language**: handheld tracking, harsh side profile, close-up of repaired hands / buckles / tools / product stress, one wide lonely frame for scale.
- **Visual style / art direction**: dust, taped metal, cracked road, patched fabric, functional bags, water marks, wind, improvised shelter, visible repair marks.
- **Color and material system**: low-saturation warm yellow, gray metal, dry fabric, scratched plastic, dust on skin, sun-baked texture, practical utility colors.
- **Lighting behavior**: hard sun, low fill, dusty backlight, high-contrast silhouettes, product readable through edge light or dust-free handled surface.
- **Sound design**: wind, cloth flap, tool click, dry footsteps, distant metal rattle, near-silence before the proof action.

Good for:

- durability, outdoor, energy, water, rugged tech, automotive stress-test concepts.

Avoid:

- using apocalypse as default "coolness".

## Output Requirement
