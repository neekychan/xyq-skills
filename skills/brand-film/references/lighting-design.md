# Lighting Design

Use this reference when writing brand-film routes or Seedance 2.0 prompts where visual texture, premium feel, product proof, or emotional pressure depends on light.

Treat lighting as a primary craft layer. A prompt that says only "premium", "cinematic", "beautiful", or "clean" is incomplete until it describes how light creates that feeling.

## Where Lighting Must Appear

Lighting should appear in two places:

1. **Style and visual reference**: define the full-film lighting rule. State the light source, direction, contrast, softness, color temperature, reflection strategy, shadow density, and how lighting changes with the emotional arc.
2. **Individual beat content**: write the lighting inside `画面内容` or the beat description. Explain how light lands on the subject, product, logo, architecture, fabric, skin, food, car body, device screen, or other story object in that shot.

## Use Lighting As Proof

- **Tension**: hard light, glare, underexposure, narrow pools of light, practical flicker, cold screen glow, heavy shadow, high contrast.
- **Release**: softened bounce, open shade, water reflection, warmer side light, cleaner fill, rim light, lower contrast.
- **Product proof**: product edge light, glass refraction, metal highlight, screen glow, condensation sparkle, fabric sheen, bottle reflection, logo catchlight, readable pack silhouette.
- **Human texture**: skin highlights, real pores, sweat sheen, face-side shadow, eye catchlight, hand highlights, fill light that protects expression.
- **Brand memory**: black background with gold logo reflection, white pack against soft shadow, neon rim on device edge, morning sun on vehicle line, warm table light on a food ritual.

## Concrete Lighting Language

Use concrete production language:

- **Light source**: sun, window, practical lamp, neon sign, screen, fire, stage light, car headlight, water bounce, mirror reflection.
- **Direction**: front light, side light, backlight, side-backlight, top light, low-angle reflection, motivated practical light.
- **Quality**: hard, soft, diffused, bounced, dappled, hazy, specular, low-key, high-key.
- **Texture result**: rim light, edge highlight, catchlight, silhouette, shadow falloff, skin sheen, glass refraction, metal glint, fabric texture, product outline.

Useful phrases:

- `侧逆光照亮喷雾边缘`
- `水面反光落在脸侧`
- `低调暗部托住金色logo`
- `产品玻璃边缘出现轮廓光`
- `柔化窗光保留真实皮肤纹理`
- `硬顶光制造正午压迫感`

## Prompt Check

Before finalizing, make sure lighting answers:

- What is the motivated source of light?
- Which side of the subject/product receives the key highlight?
- What stays in shadow and why?
- Does light change with the story, or is it only decoration?
- How does the final lockup light create brand memory?
