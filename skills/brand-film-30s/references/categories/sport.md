# Sport Brand Films

## Core Tensions

- Being told what kind of body, gender, age, class, or personality gets to win.
- The gap between public outcome and private work.
- The loneliness before the crowd sees the achievement.

## Modern Routes

- Manifesto against external doubt.
- Real-body action montage, not only heroic beauty shots.
- One athlete's pressure expanded into a wider cultural movement.

## Product Role

- Equipment as trusted companion, not hero.
- Shoe/apparel/technology appears at decisive action moments.

## Avoid

- Generic "never give up" voiceover.
- Beautiful sweat with no social or personal pressure.
