# Beauty and Personal Care Brand Films

## Core Tensions

- Who defines beauty and whose bodies are judged.
- Confidence lost through external evaluation.
- The difference between transformation and self-recognition.

## Modern Routes

- Documentary or real-person story.
- Historical brand asset reinterpreted through today's audience.
- A small human moment that reveals a large beauty standard.

## Product Role

- Care ritual, proof of agency, or object that supports self-definition.
- Product should not imply the user was insufficient before using it.

## Avoid

- "Become beautiful" logic.
- Borrowing empowerment language without brand behavior or proof.
