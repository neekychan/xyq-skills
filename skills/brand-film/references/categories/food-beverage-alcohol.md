# Food, Beverage, and Alcohol Brand Films

## Core Tensions

- Warmth, ritual, reward, hosting pressure, celebration, nostalgia, local pride, or social awkwardness.
- The product usually matters because of the moment around it.

## Modern Routes

- Occasion film: festival, game day, street, family table, bar, first delivery, after-work release.
- Heritage asset retold as a new micro-story.
- Comedy of social connection or deadpan occasion escalation.

## Product Role

- Connector, ritual object, reward, host helper, seasonal signal, or heritage proof.

## Avoid

- Product appetite shots without occasion.
- Technical production novelty that removes human warmth.
- Alcohol claims that imply performance, health, or unsafe behavior.
