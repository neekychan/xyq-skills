# Sound And Music

Use this reference when writing brand-film routes or Seedance 2.5 prompts where BGM, silence, Foley, rhythm, or sonic lockup affects the film's meaning.

BGM is not optional background decoration, but it should not always be strong, dense, or emotionally forceful. The first decision is the film's **music density**.

## Music Density

Choose one:

- **Full score**: for anthem, spectacle, broad emotion, or mass excitement.
- **Restrained score**: for controlled premium emotion or narrative lifestyle.
- **Sparse pulse**: for product beauty, luxury, thriller, precision, or tension.
- **Diegetic music**: for creator-style, culture, nightlife, social scenes, or realism.
- **Near-silence**: for restraint, trust, ritual, negative space, or sensory focus.
- **Silence-led design**: when room tone, breath, Foley, material sound, or sonic logo should carry memory.

For every film, define:

- **BGM role**: pressure, desire, suspense, warmth, speed, luxury, comedy timing, confidence, release, awe, calm, rarity, control, or distance.
- **Music style / instrumentation**: low-frequency electronic drums, strings, piano pulses, synth bass, orchestral hits, hand percussion, club beat, jazz texture, choir, analog synth, acoustic guitar, music-box motif, marching percussion, or silence-with-drone.
- **Rhythm arc**: starts minimal, drops out, accelerates, thickens, releases, stops hard, or resolves into the sonic logo.
- **Sync points**: product reveal, order placed, door opens, bottle opens, car turns, athlete jumps, UI trigger, crowd reaction, logo appears, slogan spoken.
- **Relationship to sound design**: BGM supports Foley/environment sound instead of replacing it.

## Choose Music By Commercial Type

- **Product beauty / sensory product film**: sparse premium sound bed, low-frequency pulse, close-mic Foley, glass/liquid/metal ASMR, soft synth pads, restrained strings, silence before product reveal, clean sonic logo.
- **Spectacle / VFX-led commercial**: cinematic percussion, sub-bass rise, orchestral or hybrid electronic build, silence before impact, large transformation hits, spatial whoosh, decisive final resolve.
- **Narrative lifestyle film**: warm piano/synth/guitar pulse, gentle percussion, room-tone-aware mix, music enters with the protagonist's choice and releases at the relationship/product turn.
- **Mood film**: music-first structure, loopable motif, synth texture, dream-pop, ambient bed, club beat, fashion pulse, analog tape texture, or sparse vocal fragment.
- **Brand manifesto / anthem film**: rising percussion, handclaps, choir, brass, strings, crowd rhythm, breath, footsteps, or voiceover-led score.
- **Product demonstration / stunt spot**: restrained test-bed music, ticking pulse, mechanical rhythm, timer-like percussion, tension drone, silence during proof frame, impact hit at result.
- **Format-based commercial / parody spot**: format-accurate cue first, such as news sting, tutorial bed, corporate training music, reality-show cue, trailer hit, livestream notification rhythm, or shopping-channel jingle.
- **Comedy / deadpan route**: minimal music, dry percussion, plucked bass, awkward pause, tiny stinger, or repeated musical button.
- **Thriller / noir route**: low drone, pulsing synth bass, muted percussion, string tremolo, ticking rhythm, silence drops, sub-bass hits.
- **Luxury / fashion / fragrance route**: operatic texture, sculptural strings, sparse piano, couture runway pulse, choir breath, or dark electronic glamour.
- **Documentary / creator-style route**: understated score, natural music bed, imperfect diegetic music, handheld realism, room tone, breath, occasional pulse.

## Negative Space

Choose sparse music or silence-led sound design before choosing a dense score when the brand needs restraint, rarity, confidence, taste, sacredness, precision, control, privacy, high-value decision-making, or sensory focus.

Good fit: luxury fragrance, jewelry, premium alcohol, tea, boutique hotels, wellness retreats, architecture/interior design, minimalist furniture, premium audio, high-end appliances, private banking, B2B enterprise trust, cultural institutions, art/fashion image films, selective premium travel.

Music treatment: long silence, single piano note, low barely audible drone, sub-bass held tone, distant choir breath, one bowed string, sparse analog synth, room tone, air pressure, fabric/glass/liquid Foley, short refined sonic logo.

Avoid forcing uplift when the brand's power comes from calm. Let silence, a small tonal shift, a material sound, or one sonic logo create the brand memory.

## Beat-Level Sound

In final prompts, never let `声音` mean only generic environment sound. Each timed beat should describe:

- the chosen music density or deliberate silence
- rhythm change or stillness
- key Foley or environment detail
- silence/drop if used
- how sound connects to the motion spine
- how the beat lands emotionally

Sound can continue, transform, drop out, or be answered by the next beat: tap -> chime, breath -> engine, shutter -> cut, ice -> pour, silence -> sonic logo.

## Final Lockup

The final 2-3 seconds should usually close with:

`sonic cue -> brand name or product name -> slogan / brand line`

This is not the same as full-film voiceover. Use mostly sound design during the story when appropriate, then let the end tag clearly resolve through BGM, Foley, sonic logo, and spoken closure when it fits the brand.
