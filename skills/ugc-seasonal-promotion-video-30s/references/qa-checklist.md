# QA Checklist

Before final output, check:

- Required references were read.
- Campaign occasion is identified.
- Product appears in the first 0-2 seconds.
- The first 3 seconds use a visible promotion hook.
- At least two beats show explicit promotion visuals.
- User-provided script lines are preserved when script lock applies.
- Product image lock is honored: text, logo, size ratio, package shape, color, label layout, material, SKU, and count remain consistent.
- Gifts match user images, page copy, or screenshots; unclear gifts use placeholders.
- User screenshots appear as independent screenshot cards when shown directly.
- Phone screens show one simplified platform-neutral product card, coupon card, order card, benefit card, or CTA card.
- Phone bridge returns to real product/gift/package/use scene.
- Transition method is selected from the positive transition library.
- Multi-product scenes use stable methods: one-by-one placement, pre-arranged bundle reveal, pan across stable lineup, creator pointing to arranged bundle, or split table zones. Each hand action operates one clear object.
- Short stickers are selective foreground emphasis layers. The 30s video selects 4-5 sticker moments, placed in clean safe zones with clear margins and visible foreground layering. Product, hands, face, logo, package text, and gift pile remain readable.
- Creator persona matches product category, price tier, buyer role, scene, campaign occasion, and promotion path.
- Creator expression changes with persona, product category, buyer motivation, use scenario, and script task while identity stays consistent. Expressions include concrete visible actions such as gaze, brow, smile, nod, lean, pause, and hand gesture.
- Creator styling, expression, clothing, background, props, and rhythm match the primary benefit route and campaign occasion: live-room traffic, gift mechanism, discount, coupon/full reduction, 0.01 lock, stock-up, festival gifting, brand/member day, or seasonal changeover.
- Story-skin hooks include visible emotional reversal: setup emotion, reversal emotion, product-proof belief, urgent action, and爽感/encouraging close. The product and campaign benefit are the reason for the emotion change.
- Multi-character story skins define role-specific expressions. 主角 and 配角 have different persona functions and different expression arcs that fit their roles; 富二代/霸总/闺蜜/室友/老板等配角 reactions match their character type.
- Creator appears when the concept benefits from reaction, urgency, oral recommendation, unboxing, gift reveal, or deal-hunter drama. Creator and gift pile/mechanism cards can appear in the same frame. If a creator is used, at least one early beat (0-2s or 2-5s) should normally include creator + product/benefit together.
- Product-only beats clearly state product/gift/mechanism card as the subject, but gift pile, coupon mechanism, flat lay, and bundle reveal are not forced to be product-only.
- For multiple cases, first 8 seconds differ in hook type, creator timing, scene, benefit order, or CTA route.
- User revision requests are reflected in the corresponding fields: script, persona, product image lock, gift, price, campaign occasion, CTA, transition, screenshot card, phone bridge, or storyboard.
- Voice, picture, and sticker describe the same current script task.
- Voiceover follows eight script roles: Hook, brightest offer/mechanism, mechanism/gift reveal, product proof 1, product proof 2/use scenario, campaign time + offer path, urgency/trust reminder, single CTA. Adjacent lines use different openings and verbs.
- The script does not repeat the same price/coupon/gift wording across multiple beats; one strongest offer beat is enough, then the script moves to mechanism reveal, product proof, urgency/trust, and action path.
- Product proof is compact, product-specific, and attached to deal value.
- Price, gift, stock, countdown, ranking, official subsidy, and 0.01 claims are evidence-backed or placeholders.
- Final CTA is one clear action matched to the user path: click lower link, enter live room, click product card, click yellow cart, claim coupon, or place order.
