# Finance, Insurance, and Health-Adjacent Brand Films

## Core Tensions

- Anxiety about future risk, shame, complexity, trust, or making the wrong decision.
- Users need clarity and dignity more than hype.

## Modern Routes

- Human story around the moment before or after a hard decision.
- Visualize uncertainty as weather, noise, maze, shadow, or delay.
- Use calm confidence rather than fearmongering.

## Product Role

- Guide, stabilizer, translator, safety net, or planning ritual.

## Avoid

- Guaranteed outcome claims.
- Panic-driven manipulation.
- Making vulnerable users look foolish.
