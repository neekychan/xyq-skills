# Physical Goods / Home Goods / Cleaning

Use for cleaning products, kitchen tools, home appliances, storage, pet goods, car accessories, household repair, office objects, and other tangible products where proof can be seen on an object, surface, room, body posture, or daily task.

## Buying Psychology

- Users want visible friction to disappear: dirty, messy, heavy, inconvenient, unsafe, embarrassing, slow, wasteful.
- Strong emotions: domestic pressure, being judged by family/guests, last-minute rescue, competence at home, "why did I not buy this earlier".
- The product often wins through a physical proof shot, not a speech.

## Best Formulas

- **Status Reversal**: someone is mocked for not handling a task, then product proof changes the room's attitude.
- **Efficiency Crush**: old method is slow or exhausting; the product removes the bottleneck visibly.
- **Sympathetic Misunderstanding**: a hidden household effort is misread until the product reveals the truth.
- **Expert Backing**: useful when safety, material damage, or technical correctness matters.

## Proof Forms

- Before/after surface, stain, grease, clutter, smell implication, dust, noise, weight, posture, time saved.
- Reaction proof: finger wipe, guest noticing, child/elder using it easily, side-by-side old tool failure.
- Durability proof: repeated use, stress point, water/oil/heat exposure, travel/commute scenario.

## Hook Patterns

- "客人还有十分钟到，她却把最脏的地方留到最后。"
- "婆婆刚进厨房，脸色直接变了。"
- "他被笑不会做家务，下一秒全家安静了。"
- "检查员的手指刚摸上去，老板心都凉了。"

## Example Routes

- Cleaning product: a restaurant owner has an inspection in 20 minutes; old scrubbing fails; the cleaner removes one visible greasy spot fast; the inspector's finger wipe becomes the proof.
- Storage product: roommates mock one person's clutter; after guests arrive, her space becomes the only place everyone borrows from.
- Kitchen tool: family laughs at a young husband cooking dinner; one tool makes the hard step obvious and clean; the final reaction is the elder asking where to buy it.

## Risks

- Do not overclaim impossible cleaning or repair outcomes.
- Do not make the proof too tiny to see on mobile.
- Avoid explaining materials before the viewer sees a visible improvement.
