# Seedance Positive Execution

Seedance 2.0 performs best with positive executable instructions. Describe the shot, placement, motion, transition, and continuity method.

## Positive Transition Library

Use one of these transition methods in each `camera` field:

- `direct quick cut after action completion`
- `push-in to price card`
- `pull-back to full gift table`
- `pan across product/gift lineup`
- `tilt from product to creator reaction`
- `rack focus from product to coupon card`
- `match cut on product shape`
- `match cut from package close-up to bundle flat lay`
- `cut from table flat lay to usage close-up`
- `cut from creator reaction to gift reveal`
- `cut from product placement to final CTA bundle`

## Scene-Specific Transitions

- `福利堆山`: product close-up -> direct quick cut to full gift table -> pull-back to show all items.
- `赠品逐个出现`: hand places gift A -> item settles -> direct quick cut to gift B placement -> final pull-back to bundle.
- `券/价格机制`: product close-up -> rack focus to independent price card beside product -> push-in to final price.
- `手机商品卡`: product on table -> direct quick cut to stable front-facing phone on stand -> direct quick cut back to product and gift table.
- `达人反应`: creator meme reaction -> direct quick cut to product + offer card -> push-in to coupon/gift.
- `使用证明`: table flat lay -> match cut on product shape -> usage close-up -> direct quick cut back to final CTA bundle.
- `直播间福利风格`: host holds product -> direct quick cut to one platform-neutral benefit card or product card on the desk -> direct quick cut to real product, gifts, and CTA card.

## Hand Action

Hands are functional product actions inside the shot:

- hold product
- open package
- place verified gifts on table
- point to coupon card edge
- apply product
- press button
- place item down

Scene changes happen after the hand action finishes and the product/gift remains readable as continuity anchor.

## Multi-Product Hand And Display Method

Use one clear object per hand action. The active hand action has one main target: one product, one gift, one coupon card, one phone, one package, or one tool.

To show many products or gifts, use these stable methods:

- `one-by-one placement`: hand places item A, item settles, direct quick cut to item B placement, then pull-back to the complete bundle.
- `pre-arranged bundle reveal`: products and gifts are already arranged on the table, camera pulls back to show the full set.
- `pan across stable lineup`: products stand on table/shelf in a fixed row or cluster, camera pans across them.
- `creator points to bundle`: creator points to the arranged products/gifts while hands stay near one product or one card.
- `split table zones`: main product, gifts, coupon card, and CTA card sit in separate clean zones.

When the creator demonstrates a product, the hand holds or operates one product while the other products stay placed on the table.

## Sticker Layer Method

Short stickers are selective foreground emphasis layers. Select 2-3 sticker moments for the whole 15s video. Place stickers on key memory beats:

- Hook sticker: campaign atmosphere or visual hook.
- Brightest offer sticker: final price, gift quantity, coupon, 0.01 benefit, or mechanism.
- Time/path sticker: deadline, claim path, live room, product card, or yellow cart.
- CTA sticker: one purchase action.

Product proof beats can use a clean frame with product action, texture, usage, taste, function, or try-on as the main information layer. Add a small product-proof sticker only when a specification or proof word needs to be remembered.

Sticker placement method:

- Place sticker text in a clean foreground safe zone with clear margins.
- Use upper-left, upper-right, lower-left, or lower-right empty space beside the product, or a blank wall/table area.
- Keep the sticker layer visually in front of the background and beside the product composition.
- Keep product body, product logo, package text, hands, creator face, and gift pile readable.
- Use one sticker block per selected beat, with stable rectangular or price-tag shape, strong contrast, and readable size.

## Persona-Fit Expression Design

When a creator appears, generate expression and gesture changes from the creator persona, product category, buyer motivation, use scenario, and script task. Keep the same identity, outfit, makeup, and hairstyle across beats while changing micro-expression, gaze, posture, and gesture according to the moment.

Persona-fit expression examples:

- Refined skincare / fragrance: restrained surprise, elegant interest, careful texture check, confident self-use nod, soft CTA smile.
- Practical beauty / personal care: quick deal-hunter surprise, focused use demonstration, satisfied mirror check, clear urgent reminder.
- Household stock-up / daily goods: practical excitement, quantity-checking focus, storage/use approval, family-use satisfaction.
- Food / snacks: curious smell/look, tasting delight, sharing excitement, stock-up approval.
- 3C / appliances: focused evaluation, eyebrow raise at mechanism, serious parameter/use proof, upgrade satisfaction, decisive recommendation.
- Gift buyer / relationship scene: soft anticipation, heart-flutter, warm approval, gentle inviting CTA.
- Parent / caregiver: gentle attention, safety-check focus, relieved approval, calm action reminder.
- Pet owner: playful curiosity, amused pet reaction, practical approval, warm close.

For each visible beat, write the expression that fits both persona and current task, such as `克制惊喜地看向价格卡`, `认真低头检查质地`, `尝到后眼神变亮`, `专注看参数后点头`, or `温柔笑着把礼盒推向镜头`.

## Benefit And Occasion Visual Fit

Before writing creator shots, choose the primary benefit route and campaign occasion. Use them to set expression, emotion, clothing, background, props, and pacing.

- `直播间引流`: clean creator product desk, product in hand, neutral product-card or coupon-card prop nearby, host-like posture, urgent eyes, forward lean, crisp clean top.
- `赠品机制`: gift table, vanity/unboxing props, warm or refined outfit, delighted reveal expression, hands place gifts one by one.
- `打折直降`: price card/coupon desk, practical clothing, sharper deal-hunter expression, confident nod, decisive pointing.
- `领券满减`: phone-on-stand coupon path, focused checking face, finger points to coupon edge, clear path rhythm.
- `0.01锁权益`: reservation/lock card, energetic alert expression, quick pointing, fast urgency.
- `囤货`: home storage/kitchen/bathroom/dorm shelf, casual clothes, practical excitement, quantity-checking focus.
- `节日礼赠`: gift table/date/holiday background, polished outfit, soft anticipation, heart-flutter, warm smile.
- `品牌日/会员日`: official brand counter or live desk, refined styling, restrained premium expression, member-card or points prop.
- `换季上新`: season-specific clothing and setting, outdoor/commute/vanity refresh scene, immediate-use satisfaction.

Occasion changes visual tone: 618/双11/99 uses high-energy deal room; 七夕/520 uses romantic gift tone; 年货节 uses warm family stock-up; 开学季 uses dorm/desk prep; 品牌日 uses official trust styling.

## Story-Skin Emotion Design

When the campaign Hook uses story/drama/meme language such as 剧情, 短剧, 富二代, 霸总, 反转, 逆袭, or 玩梗, use a stronger character emotion arc:

- Setup: character shows doubt, playful arrogance, being ignored, awkward pause, or misunderstanding.
- Reversal: character sees the campaign benefit/product effect and shifts to surprise, realization, jealousy, regret, impressed reaction, or sudden interest.
- Product proof: expression becomes focused and convinced while the product proof action happens.
- Time/path: character becomes urgent, points to campaign path, leans forward, or quickly checks product card/live-room path.
- CTA ending: character smiles, nods, looks satisfied, gives a small triumphant gesture, or invites the viewer to buy.

For two-character story hooks, each role has a readable emotional change: one role triggers the setup emotion, the other role reacts to the reversal. Keep the product and campaign benefit visible as the reason for the emotion change.

## Character Role Expression Method

For story-skin or multi-person videos, define each visible role before the shot list:

- `主角`: product owner/recommender/user. Expression arc shows calm or ignored setup -> confident reveal -> focused proof -> urgent guidance -> satisfied or triumphant close.
- `配角`: rich second generation, boss, friend, roommate, colleague, partner, skeptical buyer, or audience role. Expression arc follows their character: arrogant/dismissive -> surprised -> interested/jealous/regretful/impressed -> quick decision -> approving or heart-flutter close.
- `旁观角色`: if present, reacts briefly with curiosity, surprise, or agreement, then stays secondary.

Match expressions to role personality:

- 富二代/霸总: restrained arrogance, raised brow, sudden attention, impressed half-smile, decisive nod.
- 闺蜜/室友: playful doubt, widened eyes, excited lean-in, quick nod, shared smile.
- 老板/运营: nervous reveal, guilty smile, urgent pointing, relieved close.
- 家庭采购配角: practical skepticism, careful checking, approving nod, stock-up satisfaction.
- 情侣/礼赠配角: curiosity, heart-flutter, soft smile, gift-acceptance delight.

## Platform-Neutral Commerce Surfaces

Use real product scenes as the main environment: product table, creator desk, bathroom vanity, kitchen counter, gift table, brand counter, dorm desk, storage shelf, or simple commerce desk.

Show purchase paths through one stable neutral surface:

- independent product card beside the product
- coupon card on the table
- mechanism card showing price/gift/time
- CTA card near the product bundle
- phone on stand showing one simplified neutral product/coupon/order/benefit card

If the user provides screenshots, use them as independent evidence cards, tabletop printed cards, or picture-in-picture cards beside the physical product. When a phone appears, the phone screen contains one simplified card only, and the surrounding shot remains a real product or creator scene.
