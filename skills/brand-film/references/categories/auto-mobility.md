# Auto and Mobility Brand Films

## Core Tensions

- Control inside uncertainty.
- Movement as independence, duty, escape, reunion, or self-possession.
- Car as private room in a public world.

## Modern Routes

- Vehicle POV: the car sees, hears, remembers, protects, or frames the story.
- Road movie, night thriller, family journey, city choreography, weather stress test.
- Product feature becomes film grammar: camera, light, cabin silence, sensor, route, sound, charging, space.

## Product Role

- Moving space, witness, safety system, camera, companion, or control surface.

## Avoid

- Generic mountain-road beauty.
- Performance numbers without human consequence.
